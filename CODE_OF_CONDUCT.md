# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the HandParticles project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
