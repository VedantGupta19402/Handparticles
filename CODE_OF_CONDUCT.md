# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the HandParticles project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
Examples of unacceptable behaviour include:

- Harassment of any kind, sexual language or imagery
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments
- Publishing others' private information without consent

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported by contacting the project maintainer at vedantguptav@gmail.com. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response that is deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances.

This code of conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant.
